## Proposed full experiment

Retain the 1,650 eligible original training rows, excluding IDs 1384/1385. Add 1,516 unique new Accuracy-0 pairs and 1,508 unique new Accuracy-1 pairs. Final Accuracy counts: 1,558 / 1,558 / 1,558; 4,674 unique pairs. Use unweighted loss without oversampling, two epochs, effective batch 24, seed 42, and a fresh Gemma adapter, matching the previous presentation budget.

Context & Relevance labels must be assigned independently for the full dataset; this small draft is not balanced for that dimension and pairs incomplete coverage with partial accuracy. Expand beyond this pattern before training, to avoid learning a shortcut.

Keep the fixed validation set untouched and distinguish Claude agreement from human-scored performance. Exact overlap checks, duplicate/conflict checks, language/topic coverage, per-dimension class counts, provenance, and label review precede launch. Synthetic examples are research data, including deliberately incorrect statements; they are not health guidance.
